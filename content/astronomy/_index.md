title: 'Astronomy'
---

My research focuses on high-energy astrophysical phenomena and the physical processes that produce non-thermal emission.

The two main areas of my work are:

- **Theoretical modelling of non-thermal sources**, involving shock and cosmic-ray physics and numerical calculations of broadband radiative processes. These models help constrain physical parameters such as magnetic-field strengths and particle-acceleration efficiencies.
- **Planning observational campaigns and interpreting observational data**, particularly radio and X-ray observations. Radio synchrotron emission provides evidence of cosmic-ray acceleration, while X-ray observations probe extremely hot plasma and non-thermal radiation.

I apply these methods to massive stars, microquasars, active galactic nuclei, tidal disruption events, star-forming galaxies, and pulsars. My complete list of publications is available through my [ADS library](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/public-libraries/8vGw9SXzT7u1T1IWo1Ew6A).
